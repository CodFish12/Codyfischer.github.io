02 / CURRENT RESEARCH

AI-Assisted Aerospace Design

Machine Learning + Topology Optimization

My current research focuses on combining machine learning with topology optimization to investigate new approaches to aerospace component design and additive manufacturing.

I am developing a proof-of-concept workflow using generative models to produce two-dimensional structures designed around laser powder bed fusion (LPBF) manufacturability constraints.

The generated structures are then evaluated using ANSYS simulations to investigate their structural performance and manufacturability. I compare the simulation results against predictions from the machine-learning model to evaluate the model as a potential surrogate for portions of the traditional optimization and analysis process.

TOOLS: MACHINE LEARNING · TOPOLOGY OPTIMIZATION · ANSYS · LPBF · PYTHON

03 / PREVIOUS RESEARCH

Mars Crustal Magnetic Fields

MAVEN Magnetometer Data Analysis

During the previous academic year, I worked with Dr. Lon Hood on research investigating the magnetic environment of Mars using data collected by NASA's Mars Atmosphere and Volatile EvolutioN (MAVEN) spacecraft.

I worked with approximately eight years of raw magnetometer data, focusing on spacecraft passes through the Martian periapsis region below approximately 170 km altitude. The objective was to characterize crustal magnetic fields and investigate their relationship to the history of Mars' planetary dynamo and the evolution of its atmospheric shielding.

I developed quality-control plots and Mercator projection maps to process and visualize the magnetic-field data. The work required handling large scientific datasets and working with research computing environments through Bash and SSH.

I also worked with legacy scientific software and programming tools including Fortran, IDL, and SuperMongo to process, analyze, and visualize the research data.

TOOLS: MAVEN · FORTRAN · BASH · SSH · IDL · SUPERMONGO · SCIENTIFIC DATA ANALYSIS
