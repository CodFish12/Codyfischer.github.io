01 / OVERVIEW

Designing a Mobile Security System

This project challenged a four-person engineering team to design and build a functional home security robot. The system was designed around a mobile platform capable of navigating its environment while using sensors and a camera system to monitor its surroundings.

I served as the team lead and the Chief Design Engineer. I led the mechanical design of the robot and coordinated the tasks and deadlines across the rest of the team team. I gained valuable experinece in working with an interdisciplinary group and how to coordiante tasks based on an individual's skillset & desire to learn.

The project strengthened my interest in mechanical design work, and my overall intertest in engineering as a whole.

02 / Robot Design

Engineering Projects

Mechanical Design & Analysis

I led the mechanical design of the robot. I created detailed parts and assemblies to evaluate component fit, movement, packaging, and overall system integration.

I used SolidWorks motion tools to evaluate the robot's mechanical movement and verify the behavior of the drive and moving components. I also applied finite element analysis to evaluate structural behavior and identify potential issues.

I developed and maintained the bill of materials while selecting and organizing the mechanical and electrical components required to build the system.

SOLIDWORKS · MOTION ANALYSIS · FEA · BILL OF MATERIALS

Team Leadership

I led a four-person engineering team and coordinated the mechanical design with electrical, controls, and system requirements. This project was one of my first opportunities to lead a complete mechanical system while working directly with a small team.

I was responsible for more than just individual CAD parts. I had to think about how the parts moved, how they fit together, and how they interacted with other systems.
