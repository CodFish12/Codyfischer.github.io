01 / OVERVIEW

Modeling a Bridge Collapse

This project used MATLAB to model the aeroelastic flutter behavior of the Tacoma Narrows Bridge and investigate the wind conditions associated with its eventual collapse.

The goal was to take a real engineering failure and build a mathematical model capable of predicting the onset of unstable aerodynamic behavior. The simulation was then compared against historical conditions from the actual bridge collapse.

The project combined mechanical engineering fundamentals, differential equations, numerical methods, and computational modeling to connect a mathematical model to a real-world structural failure.

02 / HIGHLIGHTED PROJECTS

Engineering Projects

Aeroelastic Flutter Model

The MATLAB simulation modeled the interaction between the bridge structure and aerodynamic loading as wind speed increased. I used the model to identify the critical wind speed at which the bridge's response transitioned toward unstable flutter.

I modeled the bridge as a dynamic structural system and evaluated its response to aerodynamic excitation, incorporating wind-dependent aerodynamic effects to investigate the onset of flutter. MATLAB was used to numerically solve the governing model and examine the bridge response across changing wind conditions.

MATLAB · STRUCTURAL DYNAMICS · AERODYNAMIC LOADING · NUMERICAL SIMULATION

Model Validation

To validate the model, I compared the predicted critical wind speed against documented real-world conditions from the Tacoma Narrows Bridge collapse.

PREDICTED CRITICAL WIND SPEED WITHIN ~5% OF DOCUMENTED CONDITIONS
