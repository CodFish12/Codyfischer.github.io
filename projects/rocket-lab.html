01 / OVERVIEW

Characterizing Optical Hardware

For senior design, my team is developing a modular focal plane characterization platform for Rocket Lab Optical Systems. The system is intended to provide a flexible alternative to commercial characterization stations that can cost tens to hundreds of thousands of dollars.

The platform will allow focal plane assemblies to be evaluated across multiple optical and electrical performance characteristics, including modulation transfer function, quantum efficiency, linearity, gain states, and noise.

My role is focused on the mechanical design and integration of the system, while also leading team integration and project safety. This includes developing the mechanical architecture, evaluating requirements and design trades, building prototypes, and coordinating the interfaces between mechanical, optical, and electrical subsystems.

02 / FEATURED WORK

Modular Focal Plane Platform

A modular test platform for optical detector characterization

The system combines precision motion, optical hardware, electronics, software, and data acquisition into a single characterization platform. The mechanical architecture must maintain alignment and stability while allowing the system to be assembled, adjusted, tested, and used in a cleanroom environment.

Precision Motion Mechanical system designed around approximately 0.5 µm translation requirements and sub-pixel positioning stability.
Optical Interface Mechanical integration of the focal plane with optical components, illumination hardware, and the characterization path.
Environmental Range Designed around an operating temperature range of approximately -10°C to 50°C.
System Integration Mechanical, optical, electrical, and software subsystems must function together as one test platform.

03 / ENGINEERING REQUIREMENTS

Design Constraints

The design process begins with translating the sponsor's characterization requirements into mechanical requirements that can be designed, fabricated, and tested.

POSITIONING

Provide approximately 0.5 µm translation capability with sufficient stability for sub-pixel optical characterization.

ENVIRONMENT

Support operation across approximately -10°C to 50°C while maintaining mechanical and optical alignment.

CLEANROOM

Develop the system for compatibility with cleanroom operation and appropriate material and assembly practices.

MANUFACTURING

Design components that can be fabricated and assembled using available machining and prototyping capabilities.

INTEGRATION

Maintain defined mechanical interfaces between optics, electronics, motion components, and the focal plane.

08 / NEXT STEPS

Analysis & Validation

As the mechanical design develops, the next stage is to validate the structure and thermal behavior of the platform through engineering analysis and physical testing.

The final system will be evaluated against the original positioning, environmental, optical, and integration requirements before delivery to the sponsor.
